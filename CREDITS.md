## Base

The game began as Prototype 4 of Unity Learn's Junior Programmer pathway, Unit 4 "Gameplay
Mechanics". The idea of pushing enemy balls off a floating island, with enemies that chase the
player, a knockback power-up with a countdown and waves that grow, comes from that course.

## Removed content

`Assets/Course Library` (the course's floating island tiles, pickup and selection-ring models,
sky dome, grid textures and materials), the course scene, the three prefabs built on it and the
bounce physics material only those prefabs used were removed. The library's own licence file
states that Synty Studios provided the models for the course and not for commercial use, and
Unity Learn gives no licence to redistribute its content in a public repository. The 2021 editor
logs and per-user settings were untracked. History was not rewritten.
